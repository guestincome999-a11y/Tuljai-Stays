# Play Store Listing Draft

## Pilgrim App

App name: Tuljai Stays

Short description:

Book trusted stays near Tuljapur for a smoother pilgrimage.

Full description:

Tuljai Stays helps pilgrims visiting Tuljapur discover available lodges and Bhakt Niwas-style accommodation, request bookings, receive owner updates, and use a secure QR pass for check-in after approval.

The app is designed for simple, reliable travel planning around Tuljapur. Pilgrims can browse stays, review room details, request accommodation, track booking status, receive important announcements, and show a QR pass at check-in when their booking is approved.

Key features:

- Search Tuljapur stays
- View lodge and room details
- Request booking approval
- Track booking status
- Receive booking and announcement updates
- Show secure QR pass for approved bookings

Category suggestion: Travel and Local

Tags/keywords:

- Bhakt Niwas
- pilgrimage stay
- lodge booking
- temple travel

## Owner App

App name: Tuljai Stays Owner

Short description:

Manage Tuljai Stays bookings, QR check-ins, rooms, and guest registers.

Full description:

Tuljai Stays Owner helps lodge owners and property staff manage pilgrim booking requests for Tuljapur stays. Owners can review new booking alerts, accept or reject requests, scan secure pilgrim QR passes, manage guest register details, and keep room operations updated.

The app is intended for approved Tuljai Stays lodge owners and operational staff.

Key features:

- Receive booking alerts
- Accept or reject booking requests
- Scan pilgrim QR passes
- Complete secure check-in
- View guest register details after QR verification
- Manage room status and operations
- Track owner notifications and reports

Intended users:

- Lodge owners
- Bhakt Niwas managers
- Reception staff authorized by property owners
