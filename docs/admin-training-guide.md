# Admin Training Guide

## Dashboard

- Review booking counts, owner activity, QR status, notifications, and health signals.
- Treat dashboard anomalies as investigation triggers.

## Live Operations

- Monitor live bookings and owner presence.
- Watch pending and overdue booking requests.
- Confirm realtime events are flowing.

## Booking Control

- Search and inspect bookings.
- Use manual intervention only with reason.
- Avoid changing booking status without confirming operational impact.

## Governance

- Review lodges, owners, rooms, and photos.
- Approve only accurate and safe information.
- Record rejection reasons clearly.

## Emergency Controls

- Use emergency mode for real operational incidents.
- Use booking pause when capacity, outage, or safety requires it.
- Communicate support guidance before enabling major controls.

## Festival Mode

- Verify festival settings before peak dates.
- Confirm public banners and announcements.
- Coordinate with operations owner.

## Monitoring

- Check health, QR logs, notification metrics, and system alerts.
- Escalate degraded database or backend status immediately.

## Analytics and Reporting

- Review bookings, occupancy, QR success, owner response, and intervention counts.
- Use end-of-day reports for owner and operations follow-up.

## Incident Handling

- Classify severity.
- Notify incident commander.
- Record timeline and actions.
- Use rollback or continuity plan if approved.

## End-of-Day Review

- Pending bookings.
- Failed QR scans.
- Unresolved support tickets.
- Owner response issues.
- Incidents and action items.
