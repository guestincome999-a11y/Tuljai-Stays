# Business Continuity Plan

## Backend Outage

- Confirm outage through health check.
- Open incident channel.
- Pause new booking communication if required.
- Use manual owner/pilgrim support only for active arrivals.
- Restore service or rollback.

## Internet Outage

- Owners should use phone support path.
- Admins should record manual actions for later reconciliation.
- Avoid promising instant QR check-in if backend cannot verify.

## QR Fallback

- QR fallback requires operations owner approval.
- Verify booking through admin or support process.
- Record manual check-in reason.
- Reconcile guest register after service restores.

## Manual Booking

- Use only during approved outage or owner support scenario.
- Confirm room availability by phone.
- Record booking details outside system for later entry.
- Avoid collecting sensitive data in unsecured channels.

## Manual Register

- Follow local lodging register requirements.
- Keep paper records secure.
- Enter/reconcile in system after recovery.

## Owner Phone Confirmation

- Confirm owner identity before acting.
- Record timestamp and decision.
- Escalate disputes to operations owner.

## Admin Override

- Use with reason.
- Record incident link if applicable.
- Review all overrides end of day.

## Emergency Communication

- Use admin announcements for broad user impact.
- Keep messages factual and short.
- Update when service is restored.

## Service Restoration

- Verify health.
- Verify booking and QR smoke test.
- Reconcile manual actions.
- Send internal closure note.
