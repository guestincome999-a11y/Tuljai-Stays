# Terms of Service Checklist

This checklist must be reviewed by the business and legal owner before public launch.

## Booking and Stay Terms

- [ ] Booking request is subject to owner approval.
- [ ] Lodge/property is responsible for stay fulfillment.
- [ ] Pay-at-lodge model is clearly explained.
- [ ] Cancellation and refund foundation is described.
- [ ] Commission model is described for owners where applicable.
- [ ] Room availability can change until booking approval.
- [ ] Festival and crowd conditions may affect availability and response times.

## QR and Guest Register

- [ ] QR check-in rules.
- [ ] QR is single-use and tied to approved booking.
- [ ] Guest details unlock only after valid QR verification.
- [ ] Guest register responsibilities.

## User Responsibilities

- [ ] Pilgrims must provide accurate booking details.
- [ ] Owners must maintain accurate room and lodge information.
- [ ] Users must not misuse QR passes or submit false information.
- [ ] Owners must comply with local lodging and guest register rules.

## Platform Responsibilities

- [ ] Platform limitation of liability.
- [ ] Dispute handling process.
- [ ] Support contact process.
- [ ] Emergency/festival communication limitations.
