# Marketing Copy Foundation

## Tagline

Tuljai Stays: simple, trusted stays for Tuljapur pilgrims.

## Devotee-Focused Message

Plan your Tuljapur visit with a calmer stay experience. Find accommodation, request booking approval, receive updates, and check in securely with your approved QR pass.

## Lodge Owner Value Proposition

Tuljai Stays helps lodge owners manage pilgrim booking requests, receive timely alerts, verify QR check-ins, and keep guest register operations organized.

## Admin and Operations Value Proposition

Tuljai Stays gives operations teams a central view of bookings, lodges, owners, QR activity, announcements, platform controls, and festival readiness.

## Festival/Navratri Campaign Copy Foundation

- Plan early for Tuljapur festival stays.
- Stay updated during high-crowd days.
- Use approved QR check-in for faster reception flow.
- Owners can manage booking rush from one operational dashboard.

## Marathi Welcome Lines

- Tuljai Stays madhe aaple swagat aahe.
- Tuljapur darshan yatrekarunsathi sahaj nivas vyavastha.

## Hindi/English Short Messaging

- Hindi: Tuljai Stays ke saath Tuljapur yatra ke liye stay request karein.
- English: Find and request stays for your Tuljapur visit.

Final campaign copy should be reviewed by a native Marathi/Hindi speaker before publication.
