# Support and Contact Readiness

## Required Support Channels

| Channel                   | Status                      | Notes                                           |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Support phone             | Pending business input      | Do not use fake numbers.                        |
| Support email             | Pending business input      | Must match store listing and privacy policy.    |
| WhatsApp support          | Future optional             | Keep optional and provider-replaceable.         |
| Emergency contact process | Pending operations decision | Must define escalation owner.                   |
| Owner support             | Pending process             | Needed for lodge onboarding and booking issues. |
| Pilgrim support           | Pending process             | Needed for booking, QR, and stay support.       |
| Admin escalation          | Pending process             | Needed for operations and incident handling.    |

## Minimum Launch Requirements

- Confirm support email.
- Confirm support phone or clearly state email-only support.
- Define response hours.
- Define urgent booking escalation path.
- Define emergency announcement owner.
