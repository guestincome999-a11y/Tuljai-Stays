# Navratri Operations Manual

## 30 Days Before

- Confirm production deployment plan.
- Identify participating lodges and owners.
- Verify room inventory and pricing.
- Train owners on booking alerts, QR scanning, guest register, and checkout.
- Confirm support contacts and escalation owners.
- Prepare festival announcements and emergency templates.

## 15 Days Before

- Run staging booking and QR rehearsals.
- Verify notification delivery on real devices.
- Review owner response expectations.
- Confirm backup and rollback plan.
- Confirm admin access for operations team.

## 7 Days Before

- Freeze non-critical changes.
- Validate lodge photos and room availability.
- Confirm QR scanner devices.
- Run load and health readiness checks.
- Prepare support scripts for crowd and late check-in cases.

## 1 Day Before

- Verify backend health.
- Verify database backup.
- Verify feature flags, festival mode, and emergency mode.
- Confirm owners are reachable.
- Review command center roles.

## Launch Day

- Open command center.
- Confirm admins and support online.
- Confirm owner app devices online.
- Run booking and QR smoke tests.
- Monitor booking pressure, owner response, QR success, notifications, and support queue.

## Daily Morning Checklist

- Health check.
- Pending bookings review.
- Owner online status.
- Pending lodge/photo verification.
- Emergency/festival notices.
- Support queue review.

## Peak Booking Hours

- Watch bookings/hour.
- Watch owner response time.
- Escalate overdue booking requests.
- Use admin intervention for stuck bookings.
- Publish announcements only when needed.

## Booking Overload Procedure

1. Review booking queue.
2. Confirm owner availability.
3. Temporarily pause booking if required.
4. Send support guidance.
5. Record incident and action taken.

## Owner Not Responding Workflow

1. Contact owner.
2. Notify operations owner.
3. Admin reviews booking age and availability.
4. Escalate to reassignment or manual rejection if required.
5. Inform pilgrim through approved support channel.

## Emergency Lodge Reassignment

- Confirm alternate lodge availability.
- Confirm pilgrim consent through support process.
- Admin records intervention reason.
- Owner and pilgrim receive updated guidance.

## QR Scanning Procedure

- Owner opens scanner.
- Confirm pilgrim booking details after successful scan.
- Complete check-in.
- Verify guest register is created.
- If scan fails, use QR failure support workflow.

## Manual Booking Approval

- Use only when owner is unavailable or operations owner approves.
- Confirm room availability.
- Record admin reason.
- Notify pilgrim and owner.

## Emergency Booking Pause

- Use during capacity, outage, or safety incidents.
- Set clear public message.
- Notify support and owners.
- Review every 30 minutes until resolved.

## Festival Announcements

- Keep messages short.
- Include crowd, availability, support, and emergency guidance.
- Avoid unverified claims.

## End-of-Day Reporting

- Bookings created.
- Accepted/rejected/expired bookings.
- QR success/failure.
- Support tickets.
- Owner response performance.
- Incidents and follow-ups.

## Incident Review

- Review timeline.
- Identify root cause.
- Record customer impact.
- Assign corrective action.
- Update playbooks.
