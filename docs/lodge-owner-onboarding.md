# Lodge Owner Onboarding Guide

## Owner Registration and Login

- Owner receives approved access from operations/admin.
- Owner installs Tuljai Stays Owner app.
- Owner logs in with OTP.
- Owner confirms assigned lodge visibility.

## Room Setup

- Add room types and rooms.
- Keep occupancy, maintenance, cleaning, and blocked status accurate.
- Review festival pricing with operations before launch.

## Photo Upload and Verification

- Upload clear lodge and room photos through approved workflow.
- Avoid misleading or duplicate photos.
- Wait for admin approval before public use.

## Booking Alerts

- Keep owner app online during operating hours.
- Respond quickly to booking alerts.
- Use accept only when room is genuinely available.
- Use reject with clear reason when unavailable.

## QR Scanning and Guest Register

- Scan pilgrim QR only after booking approval.
- Do not request raw QR payload from pilgrim.
- Confirm successful backend verification.
- Complete guest register after QR check-in.
- Guest contact details should be accessed only after successful QR verification.

## Checkout

- Complete checkout in owner app.
- Update room status after checkout.
- Mark cleaning or maintenance when needed.

## Support Process

- Contact owner support for booking, QR, room, or app issues.
- Escalate urgent pilgrim arrival issues immediately.
- Record unresolved issues for end-of-day review.

## Festival Best Practices

- Keep phone reachable.
- Keep owner device charged.
- Review pending bookings frequently.
- Keep rooms updated before peak hours.
- Use support escalation before pilgrim arrives when availability changes.
